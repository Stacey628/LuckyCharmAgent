Run Evaluation Experiments with Langfuse!
Validation: does the simulator behave like real shoppers?

The shoppers are a model, not real customers. How strongly a promo sign, a shelf neighbor or a walking pace pulls on a shopper is an assumption in the simulation, and each run is one sample. Use it to form hypotheses about layout, then check them against real store data. Concepts follow Harvard GSD's Paper or Plastic: Reinventing Shelf Life in the Supermarket Landscape (shelf competitors, color and packaging salience, ergonomics, demographic targets, brand touchpoints).
